Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 August 2026

Vuka Online (“we”, “us”) builds and maintains websites and related digital services for businesses in South Africa. This policy explains what personal information we collect through vukaonline.co.za, why we collect it, and what we do with it.

Information we collect

We only collect information you choose to give us:

  • Enquiry form — your name, email address, business name and phone number, when you request a growth session.
  • Newsletter form — your email address, when you subscribe.
  • Direct contact — whatever you include when you email or WhatsApp us.

We do not run advertising trackers or analytics cookies on this site, and we do not build advertising profiles.

How we use it

  • To respond to your enquiry and discuss working together.
  • To send you the updates you subscribed to, if you subscribed.
  • To provide and support services you have engaged us for.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for their own marketing.

Who processes your data

Form submissions are delivered to us through Web3Forms, which passes the message to our email and does not retain it for its own purposes. Our email is hosted by Zoho. This site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which processes standard server request logs including IP addresses.

How long we keep it

Enquiries are kept for as long as we have an active or prospective relationship with you, and for a reasonable period afterwards for our records. Newsletter subscriptions are kept until you unsubscribe.

Your rights

Under the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA), you have the right to ask what personal information we hold about you, to have it corrected, and to ask us to delete it. You can also withdraw consent to marketing at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, or to ask anything about this policy, email hello@vukaonline.co.za or WhatsApp 072 555 4595. We aim to respond within 24 business hours.

Security

This site is served over HTTPS and form submissions are encrypted in transit. No method of transmission is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect the information you share with us.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page.
